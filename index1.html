Dashboard quality indicators

Live calculations from recorded QMS reviews, test executions, and defects.

Pre-deployment controls: review completion = completed/approved reviews ÷ all reviews × 100 (1/3 = 33%); integration test pass rate = passed executed tests ÷ executed tests × 100 (2/3 = 67%). Post-deployment monitoring: defect escape rate = defects first detected after release ÷ all recorded defects × 100 (1/3 = 33%); reopened defect rate = reopened defects ÷ closed defects × 100 (1/0 = No records). "No records yet" means add records in the relevant QMS module; formulas are not estimated.

Percentages use the left axis; open-defect count uses the right axis. Count bars are scaled for display only.
